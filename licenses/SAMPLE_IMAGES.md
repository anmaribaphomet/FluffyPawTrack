# Sample images

Demo photos bundled in `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/` for the fake
repositories (`SampleData`). All come from Wikimedia Commons under CC0, Public Domain, CC BY
or CC BY-SA. They were downscaled (640 px wide at most, re-encoded as JPEG) and some tall
photos were center-cropped; no other changes were made. CC BY-SA files keep their original
license. None of them shows the logo or branding of a real organization.
